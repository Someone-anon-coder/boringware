BoringWare makes tools for the hour spent fixing broken spreadsheet links after a server move, or
the afternoon spent splitting and renaming a month of invoices by hand.

## Why boring software

The jobs nobody wants are usually the ones nobody has built a good tool for. They are too small
for enterprise software and too fiddly for a quick macro, so people do them by hand, every month,
and make mistakes when they are tired. A tool that does one of those jobs properly is worth more to
the person stuck with it than another feature in a big suite.

## The rules every tool follows

<dl class="steps rules">
<dt>One job, done properly</dt><dd>Each tool solves a single, specific problem, and its limits are written down where you can read them before you buy.</dd>
<dt>Your files stay on your PC</dt><dd>Nothing is uploaded. The only time a tool connects to the internet is to check its licence key.</dd>
<dt>Originals are left alone</dt><dd>Results are written as new files, and nothing is replaced without asking you first.</dd>
<dt>Try it, then pay once</dt><dd>Every tool can be tried on your own files for free. Licences are a one-time price, with no subscription needed.</dd>
<dt>No AI guesswork</dt><dd>The tools follow rules you can see and change, so the same input always gives the same result.</dd>
</dl>
